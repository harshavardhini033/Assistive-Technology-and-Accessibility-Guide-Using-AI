# Capstone 78: 10-Question Comprehensive Quiz & Answer Key

**AI-Powered Assistive Technology & Accessibility Guide**

### Assessment Instructions

This 10-question assessment evaluates knowledge of WCAG 2.1 AA standards, AI accessibility tool capabilities, prompt engineering workflows, and ethical considerations in higher education.

---

### **Q1: What does the acronym WCAG stand for in digital accessibility standards?**

* **Answer:** Web Content Accessibility Guidelines.
* **Explanation:** WCAG is developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) to provide a single shared standard for web content accessibility across international jurisdictions.

---

### **Q2: What is the minimum color contrast ratio required by WCAG 2.1 Level AA for standard body text?**

* **Answer:** 4.5:1 ratio.
* **Explanation:** A 4.5:1 contrast ratio ensures that users with moderate low vision or color vision deficiencies can comfortably read normal-sized text without eye strain.

---

### **Q3: Why is complete automation (zero human review) discouraged when generating visual alt-text using AI?**

* **Answer:** Risk of AI hallucinations and technical inaccuracies.
* **Explanation:** Large Language Models can hallucinate visual details, misinterpret complex academic charts, or omit critical data, making Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) review mandatory.

---

### **Q4: Which of the following screen readers are commonly used to audit digital documents on Windows and macOS?**

* **Answer:** NVDA (Windows) and VoiceOver (macOS/iOS).
* **Explanation:** NVDA is a leading free open-source screen reader for Windows, while VoiceOver is built directly into Apple operating systems.

---

### **Q5: What is the primary purpose of maintaining a strict semantic heading hierarchy (H1 -> H2 -> H3) in course documents?**

* **Answer:** Enabling sequential, structured screen reader navigation.
* **Explanation:** Screen reader users frequently jump between headings to scan documents; proper heading tags provide the necessary architectural roadmap.

---

### **Q6: How does the 4-Step Practical Educator Workflow utilize AI Text-to-Speech (TTS) tools like ElevenLabs?**

* **Answer:** By converting written reading guides into natural, high-fidelity audio study materials.
* **Explanation:** TTS conversion creates multi-modal learning assets that benefit auditory learners, commuters, and students with visual impairments.

---

### **Q7: What is the recommended best practice when writing alternative text for a complex educational diagram?**

* **Answer:** Describing the core purpose, relationships, and data trends concisely without using redundant phrases like 'image of'.
* **Explanation:** Screen readers automatically announce images; alt-text should focus purely on the functional context and educational message of the graphic.

---

### **Q8: Which principle of the POUR framework requires that all website and document navigation be operable via full keyboard controls?**

* **Answer:** Operable.
* **Explanation:** The 'Operable' principle dictates that interface components and navigation must not require mouse-only interactions, ensuring accessibility for users with mobility impairments.

---

### **Q9: What is the primary ethical consideration regarding student data privacy when utilizing public AI models for coursework?**

* **Answer:** Avoiding the upload of student papers, grades, or personal identifiable information (PII) into public LLMs.
* **Explanation:** Educators must protect student confidentiality and restrict AI prompts to general academic materials and open-access media.

---

### **Q10: What empirical time-saving percentage was achieved by integrating AI automation into accessible content creation during Capstone 78?**

* **Answer:** Up to 80% time savings (reducing weekly prep from 15+ hours to under 3 hours).
* **Explanation:** AI automation drastically accelerates baseline alt-text drafting and transcript generation, provided that rigorous human quality checks are maintained.
